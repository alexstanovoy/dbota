Dota 2's look for bota matches: the map, heroes, buildings, trees, icons and sounds from your own Dota 2 install.

Your Dota 2 files never leave your computer. This page reads the folder you pick inside this browser tab, only the few packages it needs (about 450 MB of the install, in small ranges), and keeps a converted private copy in this browser's own storage. Nothing is uploaded, and the page has no way to send files anywhere. You can delete the copy below at any time.

Pick the dota 2 beta folder of your Steam library (steamapps/common/dota 2 beta). Your browser may list every file in it before it asks; none is read until it is needed.
